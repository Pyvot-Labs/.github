## What we do

| | Service | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 🌐 | **Web development** | Business websites, landing pages and internal tools that load fast, work on phones and are easy for you to update. |
| 📊 | **Analytics & dashboards** | Sales, traffic and ops data pulled into one dashboard. Power BI, Looker Studio, Google Analytics setup, KPI reporting. |
| 📑 | **Excel & spreadsheets** | Cleanup and restructuring, financial models and templates, macros and automation for the weekly copy-paste work. |
| 🔮 | **Data science** | Sales forecasting, customer segmentation and Python/SQL analysis that answers a specific business question. |

## How we work

1. **Tell us what's going on.** Fill in the quote form or book a free 30-minute call.
2. **Get a fixed quote.** Scope, timeline and price in writing before any work starts.
3. **We build, you check in.** Short progress updates so you always know where things stand.
4. **Handover.** We walk you through everything and stay reachable for follow-up questions.

No surprise bills, and no project is too small to ask about.
